How I think about offers

One audience. Four temperatures. One job per offer.

Cannibalisation happens when two offers do the same job for the same person. The fix isn't fewer offers. It's giving every offer exactly one job, one audience temperature and one next step.

Cold

Free content

Job
Answer "is this for people like me?"
Never
Solve their specific problem. That's what they'll pay for.
Short-form video, free events, waitlists
Warm

Entry offer

Job
Turn "interested" into "committed" with a small, specific yes.
Never
Deliver the full outcome. It should reveal the path, not walk it.
Paid assessment, audit, intensive, trial block
Hot

Core offer

Job
Deliver the transformation they came for.
Never
Compete on price with your own entry offer.
Coaching programme, engagement, done-for-you
Retained

Continuity

Job
Keep the result going, and turn clients into referrers.
Never
Be a cheaper version of the core offer.
Membership, retainer, check-ins, community

Add an offer when…

  1. The same question keeps coming up that no current offer answers.
  2. There's a big drop-off between two steps that a smaller commitment could bridge.
  3. Your core offer is at capacity and buyers are waiting.

Cut or merge when…

  1. Two offers answer the same question for the same buyer.
  2. It sells, but it leads nowhere. A dead end, not a step.
  3. You can't say in one sentence who it's not for.

Fix it, don't add, when…

  1. The offer is right but the show rate or follow-up is broken.
  2. Leads are going cold before anyone talks to them.
  3. The numbers you're deciding on haven't been cleaned.
