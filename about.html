About

I'm Pat. I learned this the hard way, in a niche that doesn't forgive mistakes.

For five years I've worked inside a regulated advisory practice, going from admin staff to business operator.

Immigration is about as high-stakes and high-trust as a purchase gets. People are betting their family's future on who they hire. You can't hype, you can't overpromise, and every claim has to stand up to a regulator. That's exactly why it taught me so much about offers and value propositions.

I built the acquisition engine, the CRM, the automation layer and the AI tooling the practice runs on today, across two countries and a team of four. The 13.4M views were the visible part. The part that made money was everything underneath.

Now I'm taking what worked and helping coaches, consultants, advisors and business owners build the same thing, without the five years of trial and error.

Questions

Fair questions.

Your results are in immigration. Does that apply to my business?

The niche is specific, the mechanics aren't. Any business where the buyer has to trust you before paying (coaching, advisory, consulting, professional services) runs on the same engine: reach new people, earn a small yes, deliver, retain. Immigration is just a particularly unforgiving place to learn it.

Do I need a big audience to start?

No. Plenty of businesses I'd work with have more leads than they're converting. If you're getting enquiries and losing them, we start with the catching, not the reach.

What does "AI in lead gen" actually mean here?

Practical, boring, useful things: scoring and routing enquiries on arrival, drafting follow-ups and client documents, summarising calls into your CRM. Always with a human checkpoint where judgement matters. No chatbots pretending to be you.

Will you build it, or just tell me what to do?

Either. Some clients want the audit and a plan for their team. Others want me to build the pipeline with them. We'll work out which fits on the first call.

How do I know the numbers are real?

The screenshots on the Results page are unedited exports from the Meta dashboard, cropped only to protect client privacy. I'm happy to walk you through the live dashboards on a call.
