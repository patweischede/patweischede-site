Cover letter

Why I'm a good hire.

Hello,

I'm Pat. For the last five years I've worked inside a 20-year family immigration practice that has helped 18,000+ clients, moving from marketing coordinator to Operations Manager and Licensed Immigration Adviser. Immigration is a high-trust, heavily regulated purchase, so I learned to grow a business without hype and with every claim able to stand up to scrutiny.

I built the acquisition engine, the CRM, the automation layer and the AI tooling the practice runs on today, across two countries and a team of four. Over 10 months that work produced 13.4M+ organic views and 111k+ followers, and three seminar campaigns with 5,600+ registrations at $0 in ad spend. The views were the visible part. The part that made money was everything underneath that caught them.

I'm looking for an in-house role in growth marketing, marketing operations or RevOps, business operations, or AI and automation. I'm comfortable owning a budget and a tech stack, managing people, and shipping automation that runs in production with a human checkpoint where judgement matters.

I'd welcome a conversation about where I can help your team.
